The intended workflow

Workflows, not overloaded resource forms.

Not every task belongs in the same resource editor. ArchivKauz will present fields and next steps suited to each object type, workflow stage and user role.

  1. Record

    Quick intake or a guided assistant with discipline-specific templates.

  2. Describe

    Add context, provenance, relationships and digital copies.

  3. Review

    Resolve completeness, rights, outstanding tasks and approvals.

  4. Share

    Present approved material in catalogues, portals and exhibitions.

Licensing & operation

Your collection. Your server.

ArchivKauz is being developed by Bitkauz as proprietary, licensed self-hosted software. The application core remains private; no open-source or source-available edition is planned.

01

Self-host

The application is self-hosted. Files can be stored locally or in your own private Scaleway S3 bucket in internal testing. Final system requirements will be published at release.

02

Activate a licence

Online activation with a test licence is implemented internally. Every future installation will require a registered licence key and online activation, including any future free licence. Official installation and update packages are intended to be available exclusively via this product website and its authorised download area.

03

Enable security updates

In internal testing, the application reports the detected domain and software version to the licence service after setup and when relevant changes occur, even when the licence binding is inactive. A security contact is optional. No collection content or visitor data is transferred. Definitive privacy information will be provided before release.

Concessionary licences are planned for eligible non-commercial individuals, associations, non-profit organisations, public museums, archives, libraries and educational institutions, with separately arranged commercial licences. Terms, durations, prices and legally binding conditions have not yet been approved.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before getting started.

Can I install ArchivKauz now?

Not as publicly available software. Internal installation with a valid test licence is now possible; customer operation has not yet been approved.

Is ArchivKauz open source?

No. ArchivKauz is being developed as proprietary, licensed self-hosted software. The application core is not public and cannot be freely redistributed.

Will free licences be available?

Free licences for eligible non-commercial users and institutions are planned, but have not been formally approved. They will also require a licence key and online activation. Pricing and commercial terms will be published later.

What happens if a licence expires or the service is unavailable?

The internal alpha supports a limited outage grace period based on signed licence evidence and read-only access with authorised catalogue export when the licence is inactive. Binding terms will be published before public release.

Is my collection data sent to Bitkauz?

Collection content is intended to stay on your server. Internal test activation and installation reporting process technical information such as the reported domain, software version and licence status, plus an optional security contact. Visitor statistics, collection content and usage analytics are not transferred for this purpose.

Is migration from Omeka S planned?

Yes. Omeka S import with mapping, validation and export is a dedicated milestone. Wendhäuser Zeitfenster is planned as the first pilot, with migration only after successful validation.
