Feature areas

One system. Many disciplines.

ArchivKauz is being developed in stages. The following areas describe the product vision; foundations already implemented internally are marked.

01Initial internal foundations

Collections & inventory

Record objects and collections, assign inventory numbers and trace changes. Loans, locations and additional inventory workflows will follow.

02Planned

Archives & discovery

Describe hierarchical holdings, account for access restrictions and create online finding aids.

03Next domain increment

Media & digitisation

Preserve original files, associate digital copies and document usage rights.

04Planned

Websites & exhibitions

Publish selected collections, create digital permanent and special exhibitions, and share knowledge.

05Further development

Metadata & authority data

Use professional standards, flexible templates and linked entities.

06Planned

Operations & preservation

Gradually integrate tasks, policies, collection care and digital preservation.

The intended workflow

Workflows, not overloaded resource forms.

Not every task belongs in the same resource editor. ArchivKauz will present fields and next steps suited to each object type, workflow stage and user role.

  1. Record

    Quick intake or a guided assistant with discipline-specific templates.

  2. Describe

    Add context, provenance, relationships and digital copies.

  3. Review

    Resolve completeness, rights, outstanding tasks and approvals.

  4. Share

    Present approved material in catalogues, portals and exhibitions.

Licensing & operation

Your collection. Your server.

ArchivKauz is being developed by Bitkauz as proprietary, licensed self-hosted software. The application core remains private; no open-source or source-available edition is planned.

01

Self-host

The application and holdings reside in your own hosting environment. Final system requirements will be published with each release.

02

Activate a licence

Every installation requires a registered licence key and online activation, including any future free licence. Official installation and update packages are intended to be available exclusively via this product website and its authorised download area.

03

Enable security updates

Installation and licence reporting is planned, including the reported domain, software version and an optional security contact, regardless of licence status. No transfer of collection content and no visitor or usage analytics. Details and privacy information will be provided before release.

Concessionary licences are planned for eligible non-commercial individuals, associations, non-profit organisations, public museums, archives, libraries and educational institutions, with separately arranged commercial licences. Terms, durations, prices and legally binding conditions have not yet been approved.

Frequently asked questions

What to know before getting started.

Can I install ArchivKauz now?

Not as publicly available software. Internal alpha test packages exist, but production activation and setup have not yet been approved.

Is ArchivKauz open source?

No. ArchivKauz is being developed as proprietary, licensed self-hosted software. The application core is not public and cannot be freely redistributed.

Will free licences be available?

Free licences for eligible non-commercial users and institutions are planned, but have not been formally approved. They will also require a licence key and online activation. Pricing and commercial terms will be published later.

What happens if a licence expires or the service is unavailable?

The concept includes a limited outage mode and continued authorised access to and export of data. The exact rules and technical measures will be documented before a public release.

Is my collection data sent to Bitkauz?

Collection content is intended to stay on your server. Licence and installation reporting is planned for technical information such as the reported domain, software version and licence status, plus an optional security contact. This reporting is separate from visitor statistics or usage analytics.

Is migration from Omeka S planned?

Yes. Omeka S import with mapping, validation and export is a dedicated milestone. Wendhäuser Zeitfenster is planned as the first pilot, with migration only after successful validation.
