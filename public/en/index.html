The vision

One system. Many disciplines.

Modules will share data, permissions and media services while providing workflows tailored to their respective disciplines.

01

Collections & inventory

Record objects, accessions, provenance, locations and loans with full traceability.

02

Archives & discovery

Describe hierarchical holdings, review access restrictions and create online finding aids.

03

Media & digitisation

Preserve masters, associate digital copies and document usage rights.

04

Public websites

Publish selected collections, design digital exhibitions and share knowledge.

05

Metadata & authority data

Use professional standards, flexible templates and linked entities.

06

Operations & preservation

Gradually integrate tasks, policies, conservation and digital preservation.

A different approach

Workflows, not overloaded resource forms.

Not every task belongs in the same resource editor. ArchivKauz will present fields and next steps suited to each object type, workflow stage and user role.

  1. Record

    Quick intake or a guided assistant with discipline-specific templates.

  2. Describe

    Add context, provenance, relationships and digital copies.

  3. Review

    Resolve completeness, rights, outstanding tasks and approvals.

  4. Share

    Present approved material in catalogues, portals and exhibitions.

Frequently asked questions

Project, hosting and licensing

Can I install ArchivKauz yet?

No. The concept is complete, but software development is still ahead. There is no installable version yet.

Is ArchivKauz open source?

ArchivKauz is planned as source-available software. Its source code will be publicly accessible. Commercial use requires a separate licence or approval; the final licence text is pending.

Who will be eligible for free use?

Individuals, associations, non-profits, public museums, archives, libraries and educational institutions within the defined usage terms, including ordinary admission and service fees. The final terms will specify eligibility.

Does my installation need to be online?

The application is designed for self-hosting. Mandatory online licence verification is planned, without sending usage or collection data. The concept includes a limited outage grace period and continued read and export access.

How can I contribute?

Documentation and the roadmap are on GitHub. Software contributions will be possible after the contribution terms have been approved.
